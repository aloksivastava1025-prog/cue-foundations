# Brag Plan: Cue Kit

## What is this app?
Cue Kit is an open-source library of 47+ Awwwards-tier React components — every one ships with the exact AI prompt used to design it, MIT-licensed, installable via the shadcn CLI. It's the free/open sibling of Cue+, built to give indie devs studio-grade motion primitives without the paywall.

## The angle
Not another SaaS explainer. A **short editorial film about the state of the web**: everything is technically impressive, yet somehow all feels the same. Cue Kit enters at the end as the answer — not sold, revealed. The video should feel like it belongs on Awwwards' own homepage, not in an ad break.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep black. One line rises: **"The web has never looked this good."** Calm, declarative, almost documentary. That confident opener is what earns the next 20 seconds — because the viewer knows a "but" is coming.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The turn** — "So why does everything still feel the same?" Held long, alone on screen. Tension before the reveal.
- **Cue Kit enters** — big display type over an indigo liquid-metal shader. Feels like a logo drop in a film title card, not a product intro.
- **Category burst** — three quick text hits over shifting gradients: `OPEN SOURCE · MIT · 47+ COMPONENTS`. Establishes credibility without a feature list.

## Outro / punchline
Return to calm. **"For websites that refuse to look generic."** Then the URL fades in: `kit.cuedesign.space`. No CTA button, no bombast — the confidence is the CTA.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only + intentional constraint (user asked: text + gradient only, no product screenshots). Story replaces the flow. All narrative weight sits on typography + shader color.

## Tone
- Preset: **cinematic**
- Creative direction: editorial × experimental web-design film — Cue Kit enters as the answer, not the ad
- Interpretation: Wide silence around each line. Big type. Two-color palette (indigo + off-white on black). Reveals are slow-and-deliberate, not busy. The video's pace comes from held silence between hits, not from motion density.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 24 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0A0A0A` (deep black — one shade darker than site white to feel filmic)
- Accent: `#5C6DFF` → deepened to `#3D50E8` for shader blend (matches Cue paid's indigo signature)
- Text: `#FFFFFF` primary · `rgba(255,255,255,0.72)` secondary
- Display font: **Inter** (500-700 weights) — matches site typography spine
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: liquid-metal / mesh-gradient shader backgrounds. Substituting the product screenshots the constraint forbids — shader becomes the "premium surface".

## Share copy (draft)
Day 27, and it's live. Cue Kit — open-source, MIT, 47+ Awwwards-tier React components with the AI prompt behind each one. kit.cuedesign.space

## Audio direction
- Role: **cinematic support** — one atmospheric bed carrying the whole 24s, one restrained low swell into the Cue Kit reveal
- Music: cinematic minimal ambient — dark editorial mood, subtle sub, no vocals, no percussion until reveal
- Music treatment: fade in from 0 over ~1s, hold at -6 dB during typography beats, small swell (+2 dB) at Cue Kit reveal (scene 3), fade out over last 0.8s
- Music cue guidance: track selected at composition time; target one strong-cue landing on the Cue Kit reveal (~9s mark) and beat-grid windows unused (typography beats are text-driven not beat-driven)
- Audio-reactive treatment: **subtle** — shader intensity may breathe with music RMS at the reveal moment only; no waveform bars, no visualizer
- SFX posture: **sparse** — at most one dry sub-hit at the Cue Kit reveal, otherwise silence around music
- Audio-coupled moments: Cue Kit reveal (scene 3) may sync to a music swell / sub-hit
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no risers, no build-up cliches; no SFX under text unless the music alone can't carry the beat

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s
Deep black canvas. Line rises character-by-character, holds full-visible for ~1.4s: **"The web has never looked this good."** Font size ~68px, weight 500, indigo tint on the period.
Sequential/interaction: yes — per-character rise, ~1.5s total entrance, then hold
Audio intent: quiet cinematic bed starts underneath, atmospheric only
Audio-coupled idea: none — text speaks first
Music: cinematic minimal ambient (starts fading in from 0)
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The turn — 5s
Black canvas continues. Line appears via mask-reveal-up, 3 phrases stacked with breathing room: **"So why does…"** then **"everything still feel"** then **"the same?"** Font size grows through the phrases (54 → 54 → 72) so the question weighs more than the setup. Long hold on the final line.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3-phrase reveal with ~0.5s stagger, full stack visible for ~2s
Audio intent: bed sustains, slight tension — no swell yet
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: continues under
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Reveal — 5s
Background shifts from black to indigo liquid-metal shader (Cue Kit's signature colour system). Big display type zooms in: **"Cue Kit."** — 200px, weight 700, tight tracking. Below, in a smaller weight, mask-reveal-up: **"The interaction library"** then **"for websites that refuse to look generic."**
Sequential/interaction: yes — shader fades in over ~0.4s, big type zoom-lands over ~0.6s, subtitles reveal at ~1.0s and ~1.6s
Audio intent: **the swell** — music lifts by ~2 dB, one restrained sub-hit lands on the "Cue Kit" landing frame
Audio-coupled idea: sub-hit on landing frame; shader intensity breathes with music RMS
Music: swells briefly, then settles back to bed level
Transition mood: dramatic wipe (indigo shader → clean black) → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Category burst — 6s
Three quick text hits over shifting gradient backgrounds. Each hit gets its own beat: **"OPEN SOURCE"** (mesh-gradient), **"MIT LICENSED"** (neuro-noise), **"47+ COMPONENTS · WEEKLY DROPS"** (warp shader). Each label ~2s: hard entrance, held ~1.5s legible, hard cut to next. Type 60-80px, weight 700, all caps, high-contrast white on darkened shader.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 sequential card cuts, each fills the frame briefly then hard-cuts to next
Audio intent: bed continues; each cut may land a soft transient (subtle low tick) but not a heavy hit
Audio-coupled idea: sequential card-arrival — bed carries the rhythm; add sparse SFX only if music alone under-scores the cuts
Music: continues at bed level, quiet
Transition mood: hard cuts within scene, then soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 5s
Return to Cue Kit's indigo mesh-gradient background but calmer. **"kit.cuedesign.space"** builds centered, character-by-character, 88px, weight 600, white. Small subtitle below, low-opacity: **"Open source · MIT · Free forever"**. Both hold for ~2s then a slow fade to black.
Sequential/interaction: yes — centered word build, ~1.2s entrance, ~2s hold
Audio intent: music slowly fades to silence over last 0.8s
Audio-coupled idea: none — silence is the punch
Music: fades to zero
Transition mood: fade to black (end)

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic minimal ambient — dark editorial, no percussion, one restrained swell at 9s mark
**Audio summary:** A single atmospheric bed rides the whole 24s. It fades in on scene 1, holds low through the question in scene 2, swells once with a sub-hit on the Cue Kit reveal in scene 3, settles back through the category burst in scene 4, and fades to silence under the URL in scene 5. Restraint is the point — no whooshes, no build-ups, no waveform tricks.
